## Description
Last night you scrolled through your messages and realized you hadn't said a word out loud to anyone all week. And you decided something is wrong with you.

It isn't. Fifty thousand years ago, a human alone for a week wasn't sad. He was dead. Your brain still runs that alarm system, and it was never switched off.

In this video:
🦴 A Neanderthal who was half blind, deaf and missing an arm, and still lived to forty
🧠 The experiment that hurt people without anyone touching them
🔥 Why a camp of three was prey, and a camp of twenty-eight survived
🔁 The loop that keeps lonely people from texting anyone
✉️ One thing you can do today, in under a minute
